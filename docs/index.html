sing-box on your phone, without the clutter

MatenLi is an open-source Android client for the sing-box core. Paste your servers, pick one, and connect. Profiles, subscriptions and routing rules stay on your device.

Download the APK Source code

Protocols

VLESS (TCP, WebSocket, gRPC, HTTP, HTTPUpgrade, Reality), VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2, TUIC, SOCKS and HTTP. Any other sing-box outbound works when you paste its JSON, for example WireGuard or SSH.

Profiles and subscriptions

Paste share links or JSON one per line, or add a subscription URL. Subscriptions refresh on demand and replace only their own servers.

Routing rules

Send domains, IP ranges and ports to proxy, direct or block. Bypass for private networks is one switch.

Live dashboard

Speed, totals, connection time, URL test and TCP ping, with logs from the core.

Config export

Copy the complete sing-box config to the clipboard at any time, for use on a computer or another client.

Material You

Dynamic colour on Android 12 and newer, or one of the accent presets. Light, dark or follow the system.

Not supported yet

xHTTP (SplitHTTP) transport. MatenLi shows a message instead of starting a profile that uses it.
